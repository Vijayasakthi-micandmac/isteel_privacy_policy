About iForce CRM & Account Provisioning

The iForce enterprise mobile application is engineered by Mic & Mac Solutions specifically for business execution, dealer network coordination, and sales operations across iSteel XLS and VIPL (Viki Industries Pvt Ltd).

iForce acts as a mission-critical Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and field execution engine connecting on-ground Field Executives with Head Office and Regional Managers to optimize daily site visits, verify dealer orders, process real-time claims, and safeguard company resources against operational discrepancies.

No Public Sign-Up • Accounts Created by Team iForce

This application does not offer public user registration or self-signup options. All user profiles and login credentials are centrally provisioned and created directly by Team iForce / authorized management administrators. Access is strictly granted to authenticated field executives and managerial staff of iSteel XLS and VIPL.

02

Core Functional Modules & Permission Justification

To ensure audit trails, eliminate manual discrepancies, and protect field staff with automated travel proofing, iForce requests explicit device permissions strictly during working duty hours:

GPS • Foreground & Background

Real-Time Live Location Tracking

Powered via Firebase and Google Maps Geolocation services. Continuous background and foreground location tracking verify genuine site visits, beat routes, and client check-ins, actively preventing route discrepancies and fraudulent allowance claims.

Camera • Visual Proof

Camera & Site Photo Documentation

Enables Field Executives to capture live, on-site photographs of construction sites, structural work, stock availability, and dealer counters for real-time reporting directly into the CRM.

Files • Financial Receipts

Expense Bill & Receipt Uploads

Enables capturing and attaching authentic fuel receipts, travel bills, and lodging vouchers for expense reimbursement processing and manager approvals.

FCM • Instant Dispatch

Push Notifications & Alerts

Delivers critical alerts, urgent announcements, and immediate manager approvals for orders, travel clearances, and expense claims.

Anti-Fraud & Tamper Verification Protocol

Location coordinates and site photographs captured through iForce include encrypted timestamps and coordinates to guarantee operational integrity for management audits across iSteel and VIPL jurisdictions.

03

Dual-Role Architecture

To ensure role segregation and principle of least privilege, data visibility and system capabilities are divided into dedicated access tiers:

Ground Tier

For Field Executives

  • Shift punches and automatic route tracking during duty hours.
  • Direct capture and submission of site proofs and dealer orders.
  • Real-time claim submissions for daily travel and food allowances.
  • Status alerts for claims approved or rejected by managers.
Supervisory Tier

For Managers & Admins

  • Live geographic visualization of active executive routes and stops.
  • Site verification portals to audit captured images before approval.
  • Instant authorization controls for claims, quotations, and special orders.
  • Automated attendance and operational deviation reporting.

All collected CRM logs, location coordinates, and audit media are processed through enterprise-grade cloud partners:

Powers low-latency live executive tracking, state management, and encrypted storage for expense receipts and site imagery.

Google Maps API

Push Messaging

05

Enterprise Data Security, Retention & Offboarding

Tracking logs, site imagery, and billing vouchers are strictly proprietary to iSteel and VIPL. No tracking information, executive records, or client details are sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized commercial entities. All data transmissions are handled through encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.

Automatic 90-Day Data Deletion Upon Leaving Job

When an employee or field executive leaves their job, resigns, or is offboarded by iSteel or VIPL, their account access is immediately deactivated by system administrators. All associated profile details, device tokens, and operational tracking data are automatically and permanently deleted within 90 days from production databases, maintaining strict data minimization and regulatory hygiene.

End-to-End Encryption

All data transit uses TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Media and audit proofs are securely signed with authorized tokens.

Strict Access Controls

Role-based authorization prevents field executives from accessing other territories or supervisory review logs.

Zero Public Self-Signup

Self-registration is disabled. Every user is authenticated, vetted, and created solely by Team iForce.

Automated 90-Day Purge

Scheduled database lifecycle jobs permanently remove departed employee records after 90 days.
